# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant] as its
code of conduct. The full text is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In short:

- Participate in a way that makes this a welcoming project for everyone,
  regardless of background or experience level.
- Give and accept feedback about the code, not the person. Technical
  disagreement is expected and healthy; contempt is not.
- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private
  information, and other unprofessional conduct are not acceptable in any
  project space — issues, pull requests, discussions or elsewhere.

These standards apply to everyone in the project's spaces, and to anyone
officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers by opening a
[GitHub Security Advisory][report] (which is private) or contacting a maintainer
directly. Reports are handled confidentially, and reporters are not required to
identify themselves publicly.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take
appropriate corrective action — from a private warning through to a permanent
ban from project spaces — in response to behaviour they deem inappropriate.
Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the code of conduct in good faith may
face consequences determined by the other maintainers.

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
[report]: https://github.com/adikeshri/valyria/security/advisories/new
